Joint Observation Preprocessing and Feature-Group Ablation for State Representation Optimization in Reinforcement Learning-Based Propofol Control

Kahyun Kim, Hyojun Ahn, Hyung-Chul Lee, and Joongheon Kim

Paper link forthcoming

We examined how BIS observation preprocessing and state representation affect PPO-based propofol control. In a reconstructed PK–PD simulator, four conditions were compared using held-out VitalDB case profiles and simulated BIS outcomes.

Research conducted in Prof. Joongheon Kim’s lab, Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Korea University.
